Lehan Zhang: homepage

This website comes in five versions: a standard academic site and four whimsical ones. They all hold the same content (research, talks, CV, art and more), so pick whichever you like. The links under each one jump straight to a page or a moment inside it.

This computer asks websites to reduce motion, so the glowing, pulsing and wiggling in the fun versions is switched off: that is what visitors with this setting will see. (In Windows: Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects › Animation effects.)
