Latin Modern Sans (subsets) for the Overleaf version's slide preview
====================================================================

These are Latin-only subsets of the Latin Modern Sans fonts (lmsans8, lmsans9, lmsans10, lmsans12),
the fonts pdfLaTeX uses for the beamer slides, so that the preview looks like the compiled PDF.

Changes: cut down to Latin characters and converted to WOFF2 (2026-10-02). As the licence asks of
modified versions, the files carry new names (-subset). They are used only by overleaf.css.
